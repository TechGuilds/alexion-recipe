# Unmappable behavior

Identify the precise source construct, observed behavior, affected routes and why the target cannot yet represent it. Distinguish missing evidence from a known unsupported feature.

Offer recovery, a bounded replacement, later resolver work, explicit exclusion or a labelled temporary stub. Explain user-visible impact before approval. Never silently remove forms, authentication, personalization, analytics, media behavior or server-side data shaping.

Record the chosen scope and unresolved hazard by entity ID. A stub can support incremental rendering but cannot satisfy functional completion. Revisit dependent tasks when a fallback changes.
